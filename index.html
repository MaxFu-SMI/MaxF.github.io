Unity URP · Shaders

Ink Arm Shader

A custom metaballs shader in Unity’s Universal Render Pipeline that renders a skeletal arm, from the arm bones to the fingertips, as an inky liquid while keeping the bones recognizable.

  • Each bone is a separate object, chained from the arm to the fingertips
  • Extensions in progress: per-ray segment culling, drips and sag, impact ripples, and a DepthNormals pass
